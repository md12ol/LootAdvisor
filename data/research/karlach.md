# Karlach – LootAdvisor research (BG3 Patch 8 + hotfixes)

Character: **Karlach**, Zariel Tiefling (locked race: fire resistance, darkvision), default Barbarian (Outlander), default subclass Berserker at L3.
Base stats (L1, Balanced): STR 17, DEX 13, CON 15, INT 8, WIS 12, CHA 10. Proficiencies: light + medium armour, shields, simple + martial weapons. **No heavy armour proficiency.** A Fighter dip later in the run grants light/medium/shields only (NOT heavy) — bg3.wiki Proficiency page. Non-proficient armour = disadvantage on attacks, ability checks and saves.
Infernal Iron note: Dammon (Emerald Grove, Act 1) uses the first Infernal Iron for her engine upgrade; at Last Light Inn (Act 2) further irons give the 2nd engine upgrade and then, in order, **Flawed Helldusk Armour → Flawed Helldusk Helmet → Flawed Helldusk Gloves** (bg3.wiki Infernal Iron). There are 4 irons in Act 1, 3 in Act 2, more in Act 3 (Huido sells one for 1,000 gp).

Source reliability: item stats/locations come from bg3.wiki (primary). Build picks come from eip.gg, gamestegy, vaagame, hacktheminotaur, thegamer/deltia summaries and r/BG3Builds threads (summarised). Where a summary contradicted the wiki (e.g. Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength "sets STR 21") the wiki value is used (it is 23).

Mechanics that drive item value (bg3.wiki):
- Tavern Brawler (Patch 8): adds STR mod to attack AND damage of unarmed, **Throw** (also throwing non-thrown weapons/objects) and improvised attacks. Honour Mode: improvised melee get no extra damage, thrown creatures take no TB damage on landing; Throws keep both bonuses.
- Giant's Rage: rage damage bonus doubled on Throw attacks (NOT all damage doubled — several guides overstate this).
- "+1d4 to Throw" items (Ring of Flinging, Gloves of Uninhibited Kushigo) apply to every thrown thing (weapons, objects, creatures). Weapon-damage riders (Caustic Band, Helldusk Gloves, Dark Justiciar Gauntlets, Horns of the Berserker) also apply to thrown weapons.
- Raising STR is double value for Tavern Brawler (attack + damage on both TB and normal STR). STR setters: Club of Hill Giant Strength (19), Elixir of Hill Giant Strength (21), Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength (23), Elixir of Cloud Giant Strength (27). Setters don't stack — the highest wins; drinking Cloud Giant frees the glove slot.
- Known bug: throwing from stealth / as the opener can leave the weapon un-returned — open combat first.

## 4. Synergy sets

### Act 1
1. **"Pike Flinger"** — THROWZ / GIANT (pre-L6)
   Main: Returning Pike · Ranged: Titanstring Bow · Head: Haste Helm · Chest: Adamantine Scale Mail · Gloves: Gloves of Uninhibited Kushigo · Boots: Disintegrating Night Walkers · Amulet: Amulet of Misty Step · Rings: Ring of Flinging + Caustic Band · Consumable: Elixir of Hill Giant Strength.
   Why: every Throw/Enraged Throw gets Tavern Brawler STR + 1d4 (ring) + 1d4 (gloves) + 2 acid; Misty Step ×2 and Momentum give throwing angles/high ground; STR 21 elixir boosts TB twice.
2. **"Wrath Cleaver"** — GWM Berserker
   Main: Jorgoral's Greatsword (or Everburn Blade/Sussur Greatsword) · Head: Cap of Wrath · Chest: Bloodguzzler Garb (unarmoured) or Adamantine Scale Mail · Gloves: Gloves of the Growling Underdog · Boots: Linebreaker Boots · Amulet: Periapt of Wound Closure · Rings: Crusher's Ring + Caustic Band.
   Why: three independent Wrath triggers (damaged, ≤50% HP, Dash) keep +damage up; Underdog advantage offsets GWM -5.
3. **"Adamantine Wall"** — WILD-B / sword-and-board thrower
   Main: The Blood of Lathander · Off: Adamantine Shield · Chest: Adamantine Scale Mail · Boots: Boots of Speed · Amulet: Periapt of Wound Closure · Rings: Ring of Protection + Caustic Band.
   Why: double crit immunity is redundant but the Reeling-on-miss from both pieces + Bear resistances makes her nearly unkillable; Lathander self-revive.
4. **"Early STR Brawler"** — GIANT/THROWZ unarmed/object throwing
   Off-hand: Club of Hill Giant Strength (STR 19) · Gloves: Kushigo · Rings: Ring of Flinging · Elixir of Hill Giant Strength when needed.
   Why: TB throws of objects/creatures before Returning Pike is bought.

### Act 2
1. **"Storm Jabber"** — GIANT
   Main: Lightning Jabber (returns via Elemental Cleaver from L6) · Head: Dark Justiciar Helm or Flawed Helldusk Helmet · Chest: Adamantine Scale Mail (or Yuan-Ti Scale Mail with DEX 16) · Gloves: Kushigo · Boots: Disintegrating Night Walkers · Cloak: Cloak of Protection · Rings: Ring of Flinging + Shadow-Cloaked Ring (Act 2 is permanently dark/shadow-cursed → frequent obscured targets).
   Why: thrown hits carry 1d4 lightning + Cleaver 1d6 + doubled rage bonus + 1d4+1d4 throw riders.
2. **"Pike Frenzy"** — THROWZ
   Main: Returning Pike · Gloves: Kushigo · Rings: Ring of Flinging + Risky Ring · Amulet: Surgeon's Subjugation Amulet · Boots: Evasive Shoes · Elixir of Bloodlust.
   Why: Risky Ring advantage → more hits and crits; Surgeon's paralyse on a crit makes follow-up hits auto-crit; Bloodlust adds an action on kill.
3. **"Vigilant Reaper"** — GWM / WILD-T
   Main: Halberd of Vigilance · Gloves: Flawed Helldusk Gloves (or Dark Justiciar Gauntlets) · Head: Flawed Helldusk Helmet · Rings: Risky Ring + Killer's Sweetheart · Amulet: Surgeon's Subjugation Amulet · Cloak: Cloak of Protection.
   Why: reach + initiative + advantage on reaction attacks (Polearm Master/Sentinel style), elemental riders, guaranteed crits for GWM spikes.
4. **"Mighty Unarmoured"** — WILD-B / GIANT unarmoured
   Chest: The Mighty Cloth (STR 20, immune to push) or Enraging Heart Garb (CON+2, Wrath in rage) · Gloves: Bracers of Defence (+2 AC) · Cloak: Cloak of Protection · Boots: Evasive Shoes.
   Why: Unarmoured Defence scales with CON; Bracers add +2 AC only without armour/shield.

### Act 3
1. **"Thunder Throwzerker"** — THROWZ (BiS-style)
   Main: Nyrulna · Off: Viconia's Walking Fortress · Head: Horns of the Berserker (or Helmet of Grit) · Chest: Helldusk Armour (or Bhaalist Armour for piercing vulnerability — WARNING) · Gloves: Helldusk Gloves or Legacy of the Masters (with Elixir of Cloud Giant Strength) · Boots: Helldusk Boots · Cloak: Cloak of Displacement · Amulet: Amulet of Greater Health · Rings: Ring of Flinging + Caustic Band (skip Shadow-Cloaked Ring — Nyrulna emits light).
   Why: each Throw = Nyrulna 1d6+3 + 1d6 thunder + 3d4 AoE + TB STR(27) + 1d4 Flinging + 2 acid + glove rider; Action Surge burst turn; Helldusk set adds immunity/forced-movement protection.
2. **"Giant's Colossus"** — GIANT (melee/throw hybrid) & GWM
   Main: Balduran's Giantslayer (Giant Form) · Head: Helm of Balduran · Chest: Helldusk Armour · Gloves: Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength (or Legacy of the Masters + Cloud Giant elixir) · Boots: Helldusk Boots · Cloak: Cloak of Displacement · Amulet: Amulet of Greater Health · Rings: Killer's Sweetheart + Ring of Regeneration · Consumable: Elixir of the Colossus.
   Why: Giantslayer doubles STR damage bonus → STR items are worth twice; Giant's Rage + Giant Form + Colossus size stack (Giant Form does not stack with Enlarge spell per wiki); crit immunity + regen + damage reduction tank.
3. **"Bonespike Berserker"** — WILD-B / unarmoured GIANT
   Head: Bonespike Helmet · Chest: Bonespike Garb · Gloves: Bonespike Gloves (MISSABLE) · Boots: Bonespike Boots · Amulet: Amulet of Greater Health · Cloak: Cloak of Protection · Rings: Ring of Regeneration + Ring of Free Action · Main: Sword of Chaos or Balduran's Giantslayer.
   Why: every Rage = 15 temp HP + 2d4 psychic aura; CON 23 powers Unarmoured AC (10+DEX+6, +1 Bonespike Boots) and Garb retaliation (6 piercing); resistance-ignore gloves.
4. **"Executioner"** — WILD-T / THROWZ crit variant
   Main: Nyrulna (or Returning Pike) · Off: Bloodthirst (off-hand Exploit Weakness = piercing vulnerability) · Chest: Bhaalist Armour (WARNING) · Head: Sarevok's Horned Helmet (crit 19–20) · Gloves: Craterflesh Gloves (WARNING) · Rings: Killer's Sweetheart + Risky Ring · Amulet: Surgeon's Subjugation Amulet.
   Why: piercing vulnerability doubles piercing hits; widened crit range + advantage + guaranteed crit after kills.
5. **"Hellfire Cleave"** (thematic, still strong) — GWM Berserker
   Main: Hellfire Greataxe · Chest: Helldusk Armour · Gloves: Helldusk Gloves · Boots: Helldusk Boots · Head: Helm of Balduran (Helldusk Helmet only with Heavy proficiency) · Amulet: Amulet of Greater Health.
   Why: fire riders + Fly + teleport; Karlach's innate fire resistance and her Infernal Fury fire damage fit the theme.

## 5. Community consensus notes

- **Widely called BiS for Karlach (melee)**: Balduran's Giantslayer, Helldusk Armour (self-proficient heavy armour — the reason it's "made for" Karlach), Helm of Balduran, Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength, Helldusk Boots, Amulet of Greater Health, Cloak of Displacement. (eip.gg, many guides.)
- **Widely called BiS for throwers**: Ring of Flinging + Gloves of Uninhibited Kushigo + Returning Pike (Act 1–2) → Nyrulna (Act 3). These four items are named in essentially every Throwzerker/Giant thrower guide and r/BG3Builds threads ("Nyrulna Throwbarian", weekly Barbarian discussion). Caustic Band and Shadow-Cloaked Ring are the common second rings.
- **Act 2 thrower weapon disagreement**: gamestegy/vaagame/hacktheminotaur say Lightning Jabber (for GIANT, which returns it via Elemental Cleaver); THROWZ players mostly keep the Returning Pike since Jabber does not return without Cleaver/Weapon Bond. Gamestegy also suggests Shar's Spear of Evening (story-locked).
- **Armour disagreement**: Helldusk Armour (consensus) vs unarmoured Bonespike Garb + Amulet of Greater Health (Bear/Giant fans) vs Bhaalist Armour (piercing throw maximisers; requires the Murder Tribunal path). Yuan-Ti Scale Mail and Armour of Agility are often recommended but are worse for Karlach's default DEX 13 than Adamantine Scale Mail.
- **Gloves**: Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength are "BiS" in guides that don't use elixirs; optimisers instead drink Elixir of Cloud Giant Strength (27) and use Legacy of the Masters (+2/+2) or Helldusk Gloves (+1d6 fire per hit; bugged +1 to all attacks).
- **Helmet**: Helm of Balduran (defence) vs Horns of the Berserker (damage, also boosts throws). Helldusk Helmet appears in several Karlach guides, but bg3.wiki lists it as a heavy helmet → proficiency problem for Barbarian unless Heavily Armoured is taken. Flag for game-data check.
- **Niche/overrated**: Dwarven Thrower (its bonus is dwarf-only; Karlach gets a plain +2 returning hammer), The Sparkle Hands (unarmed-only charges), Thermodynamo Axe (theme pick), Club of Hill Giant Strength (only until elixirs/other STR).
- **Overstated mechanics in guides**: Giant's Rage doubles only the RAGE damage bonus on throws (wiki), not all thrown damage. Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength set STR 23 (eip claimed 21).
- **Nyrulna caution**: its 6 m thunder blast damages allies/neutrals; keep a non-AoE throwable (Returning Pike/Lightning Jabber) on the hotbar for crowded fights. Throwing from stealth can bug the return.
- **Patch 8 Honour Mode**: Tavern Brawler throws keep attack+damage bonus; improvised melee loses the damage part; thrown creatures don't take TB landing damage.
