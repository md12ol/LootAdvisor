# LootAdvisor research — crafted, forged, assembled and multi-step items (BG3 Patch 8)

Scope: every item that needs more than "walk up and loot it": forge recipes, assembled
pieces, puzzles, keys, multi-stage quests, story-choice rewards, and build-defining
elixirs. Written in our own words from bg3.wiki (primary), cross-checked with Game8,
Fextralife and other guides (URLs at the end).

Conventions
- **Item names** are written exactly as bg3.wiki spells them (intended to match game data).
- Coordinates are bg3.wiki "X: Y:" map coordinates. Some sub-levels (Rosymorn Monastery
  interior, House of Hope, Sorcerous Vault, Shadowfell) use their own coordinate frame.
- **WARNING:** marks a kill, theft, hostility, broken oath, lost companion or other cost.
- **MISSABLE:** marks a timing window.
- **Do:** = the 1–2 sentence "what to do" note for the advisor UI.

## ACT 1

### 1. Adamantine Forge (Grymforge / Abandoned Refuge, Underdark)

Only **two Mithral Ore** exist per playthrough, so at most **two** adamantine items can be
forged. Each mould can be used repeatedly (it stays in the forge until ejected), so
two copies of one item are possible.

#### Forge recipes (mould + Mithral Ore → item)

| Mould (exact) | Result (exact) | Rarity | Short effect | Mould location |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Splint Mould | **Adamantine Splint Armour** | Very rare | Heavy, AC 18; no crits against you; all damage −2; melee attackers who hit you are Reeling 3 turns; Stealth disadvantage | Grymforge near Ancient Forge waypoint, on a rock ledge above lava pools — X: -593 Y: 311 |
| Scale Mail Mould | **Adamantine Scale Mail** | Very rare | Medium, AC 16 + Dex (max 2); no crits; all damage −1; melee attackers Reeling 2 turns | Near Ancient Forge waypoint, on the floor beside a standing torch — X: -618 Y: 264 |
| Shield Mould | **Adamantine Shield** | Rare | +2 AC; no crits; Shield Blow reaction (Dex save or Prone); melee misses make attacker Reeling | On a skeleton at the very top of the Dormitory stairs, next to the room with the Merregon Legionnaire and Hellsboars — X: -555 Y: 408 |
| Longsword Mould | **Adamantine Longsword** | Rare, +1 | Hits on objects always crit (Diamondsbane); ignores Slashing resistance | High above the central chamber on an overhanging metal bridge; reach via stairs in the northernmost part of Grymforge — X: -626 Y: 406 |
| Mace Mould | **Adamantine Mace** | Rare, +1 | Object crits; ignores Bludgeoning resistance | Near Ancient Forge waypoint — X: -614 Y: 283 |
| Scimitar Mould | **Adamantine Scimitar** | Rare, +1 | Object crits; ignores Slashing resistance | On a skeleton in a ruined stairwell with two levers, overlooking the rubble that traps Nere; needs a jump or Misty Step — X: -604 Y: 322 |

#### Mithral Ore (2 total)
1. **Mithral Ore #1** — north-east of the Adamantine Forge, across lava against the cavern wall (library/archway area) — X: -556 Y: 277.
2. **Mithral Ore #2** — north-west of the Abandoned Refuge / south-west of the Ancient Forge waypoint, across lava beside a cliff — X: -642 Y: 253.
Both need lava crossing: jump, Misty Step, Fly, or Fire resistance/immunity.

#### Steps in order
1. **Reach Grymforge**: skiff at the Decrepit Village beach dock in the Underdark (X: -7 Y: -210) sails across Ebonlake to Grymforge.
2. **Get past the rubble** (quest "Get Past the Rubble"/"Free True Soul Nere") to open the upper Abandoned Refuge — see §2 below for explosives.
3. Cross the iron grate walkway south, use the two lever-controlled platforms over the lava chasm, unlock waypoint **Underdark - Ancient Forge** (X: -611 Y: 294).
4. Collect the moulds you want and both **Mithral Ore**.
5. At the **Adamantine Forge** (Grym stands on it, around X: -558 Y: 230): put the ore and mould into their two slots → pull the lever to drop the hammer (prepares ore) → **open the lava valve** (this wakes **Grym** the first time) → drop the hammer again to forge → use the lever to eject the finished item / mould.
6. Raising the platform blocks further forging until it is lowered again.

#### Grym fight (mandatory on first valve use)
- Grym (Adamantine golem) is immune to all damage until **Superheated** by standing in lava; then it is vulnerable to Bludgeoning.
- Best method: lure it onto the central anvil (it always attacks the last creature that hit it — "Prime Target"; Minor Illusion also works) and drop the forge hammer for 12d8 Bludgeoning.
- Magma Mephits join. Ranged attacks from high ground are safe.
- Drop: **Grymskull Helm** (Very rare: no crits against wearer, Fire immunity, Hunter's Mark 1/LR). 875 XP. Achievement "A Grym Fate" if killed without the hammer.
- **Do:** Grab both Mithral Ore first, then forge your best item (Splint for heavy tanks, Scale Mail for medium, Shield for any shield user); kill Grym with the hammer trap.

### 2. Disintegrating Night Walkers (Nere, Grymforge)
- **Disintegrating Night Walkers** — boots, Story Item tag (bug), Misty Step 1/SR; immune to web/entangle/ensnare and Grease/Ice slips.
- Steps:
  1. Grymforge: Nere is trapped behind a cave-in at X: -635 Y: 311.
  2. Explosives: **Runepowder Vial** from Philomeen (hidden button near X: -584 Y: 416, DC 10–15 Persuasion) **or** 2× **Smokepowder Satchel** from Philomeen's hidden reserve (map at X: -539 Y: 464), or a Smokepowder Barrel / Force damage (rubble has 44 HP, Toughness).
  3. Blow the rubble; Nere comes out.
  4. **WARNING:** to get the boots you must **kill Nere** (and usually his duergar/True Soul allies). Loot: 1000 gp, **Disintegrating Night Walkers**, **Sword of Screams**, **Broken Moonlantern**, **Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen**. Persuading him to leave the cult gives only a Dagger +1.
- **Do:** Free Nere, then kill him for the boots — excellent on any melee or caster that hates difficult terrain.

### 3. Sussur weapons — "Finish the Masterwork Weapon"
- Results (all **Rare, +1**, silence on hit, DC 12 CON, 2 turns, melee hits only): **Sussur Dagger**, **Sussur Sickle**, **Sussur Greatsword**.
- Steps:
  1. Start: read **Highcliff's Journal** or **Highcliff's Blueprints** in Blighted Village houses (Act 1 surface).
  2. Underdark → **Dread Hollow**: climb the big Sussur Tree, take **Sussur Tree Bark** (X: -14 Y: -138; waypoint at the tree base).
  3. Return to the Blighted Village blacksmith forge (Whispering Depths / smithy, X: -446 Y: -378). Work the bellows to light the furnace, add the bark (flames turn blue).
  4. Add a plain **Dagger**, **Greatsword** or **Sickle** (a dagger and greatsword lie in the same room). One bark = one weapon.
- **Sussur Bloom** (not a weapon): blue flower, creates an antimagic field (no spellcasting); 4 around the Sussur Tree, 2 in the Arcane Tower garden. Used to shut down the Arcane Tower defences (Animated Armour / Turrets). Wilts if carried out of the Underdark unless stored in the Traveller's Chest.
- **Do:** Pick the bark up on your first Underdark visit; Sussur Greatsword is the best pick for a caster-hunting melee character.

### 4. Phalar Aluve (Underdark)
- **Phalar Aluve** — Rare +1 longsword, Finesse(!), versatile; Sing (allies +1d4 attack) / Shriek (enemies −1d4) toggles, +1 Performance.
- Location: embedded in a rock in the Underdark, X: 116 Y: -192.
- Pull it out by one of: DC 15 Strength; DC 15 Religion (Lolth cleric: advantage); Paladin DC 10 Religion/Charisma; Cleric of Eilistraee automatically; or spill blood on the stone (any damage/ritual dagger) to skip checks.
- **Do:** Free it with a strong character or by bleeding on the stone; great for Dex melee bards/rogues.

### 5. Blood of Lathander (Rosymorn Monastery, Mountain Pass)
- **The Blood of Lathander** — Legendary +3 mace. Once per LR revive at 0 HP to 2d6 HP and heal allies 1d6; light aura that blinds fiends/undead (CON DC 14); Sunbeam 1/LR.
- Steps (quest "Find the Blood of Lathander"; monastery interior coordinates):
  1. Start: pick up the **Ceremonial Mace** from a Kobold Looter (X: 40 Y: 28, monastery ground floor) — or at Dawnmaster Stockhold's grave (X: 127 Y: -5).
  2. **Ceremonial Battleaxe**: monastery roof (X: 95 Y: 46), guarded by a Guardian of Faith.
  3. **Ceremonial Warhammer**: Giant Eagle nest, top floor (X: 54 Y: 55 or X: 89 Y: 41).
  4. Put each on its Dawnmaster's table: warhammer → Seed, mace → Stockhold, battleaxe → Vaseid. A hidden wall opens: take the **Dawnmaster's Crest**.
  5. Go down through the crumbling stairs south of the stained-glass room to the Crèche Y'llek basement (X: 74 Y: 80). In the west hall (X: 1330 Y: -660) rotate the two statues: south statue faces west, north statue faces east (DC 25 Athletics each, or Grease tricks).
  6. Secret chamber, locked altar (Crèche Y'llek X: 1068 Y: -779): read the plaque under Lathander's statue, then **use the Dawnmaster's Crest** on the altar — mace is released safely.
  - Re-entry tip: broken windows at X: 64 Y: 31; lever at X: 78 Y: 49 opens the main doors.
- **WARNING:** Taking the mace **without** the Crest arms a 4-turn trap: either escape via the energy portal to the roof or destroy 4 Lathander solar machines; failing destroys Crèche Y'llek (and everyone in it) and turns the githyanki hostile.
- **MISSABLE:** Act 1 only.
- **Do:** Get the Crest first, then claim the mace — top-tier mace/holy item for Clerics, Paladins and anyone who wants a free death-save.

### 6. Circlet of Psionic Revenge (Crèche Y'llek)
- **Circlet of Psionic Revenge** — Rare helm: when you succeed on a save, the source takes 1d4 Psychic; githyanki get +1 INT/WIS/CHA saves.
- Carried by Inquisitor Ch'r'ai W'wargaz in the Inquisitor's Chamber (X: 1392 Y: -752).
- **WARNING:** requires killing the Inquisitor (Lae'zel's quest "The Githyanki Warrior"/"Find a Cure" fight) or a pickpocket; killing him turns the crèche hostile unless done in the quest confrontation.
- **Do:** Good for Lae'zel (githyanki bonus); take it when the Inquisitor fight happens.

### 7. Auntie Ethel's Hair (Riverside Teahouse → Ancient Abode)
- **Auntie Ethel's Hair** — consumable, permanent +1 to an ability score of your choice ("Auntie Ethel's Boon").
- Steps: follow Ethel into the Overgrown Tunnel / Ancient Abode (rescue Mayrina) → fight her → drop her below ~20% HP → she begs → accept her deal; pass DC 20 Deception or Intimidation (DC 15 with advantage for Fighter/Barbarian) to also get Mayrina freed.
- **WARNING:** Paladins breaking oath; Ethel escapes (no kill loot that run, she returns in Act 3 where she can drop **Ring of Feywild Sparks**).
- **Do:** Take the hair (odd-score fix, e.g. 17→18 for a main stat); skip if you are a Paladin who wants to keep the oath.

### 8. Infernal Iron (Act 1 pieces) — feeds Karlach's engine and Dammon's Flawed Helldusk set (Act 2)
- Act 1 locations:
  - Blighted Village forge basement chest — X: -434 Y: -370 (with the Steelforged Sword).
  - Shattered Sanctum, Dror Ragzlin's treasure (locked room above the throne) — X: 298 Y: 66.
  - Zhentarim Hideout (Waukeen's Rest basement) storage room trapped chest — X: 282–285 Y: -165/-174. Iron gate opens with **Zhentarim Key** (Zarys or Vol) or lockpick; chests DC 10 Sleight of Hand to disarm or **Ornate Key** (Vol). **WARNING:** opening it without stealth makes the Zhentarim hostile (Jarg, Karad, Vol + wolves).
  - Grymforge: Stonemason Kith (X: -662 Y: 375), reward/sale if checks pass.
- Karlach: Dammon (Emerald Grove Hollow X: 180 Y: 561) upgrades her engine — 1st upgrade **Heart of Iron**, 2nd (after a long rest, Act 2 Last Light Inn or Act 3 Forge of the Nine) **Heart Ablaze**. Two Infernal Iron total.
- **WARNING:** Dammon dies if the grove/refugees are lost (Raid the Emerald Grove) or Last Light falls — then no engine upgrades and no Flawed Helldusk set.
- **Do:** Hoard every Infernal Iron; save the tieflings so Dammon survives.

### 9. Dark Urge: The Deathstalker Mantle
- **The Deathstalker Mantle** — Rare cloak: once per turn when you kill an enemy, become Invisible for 2 turns.
- Steps (Dark Urge origin only): at camp the Urge pushes you to kill **Alfira** (or Quil if Alfira isn't there) → after the murder, long rest → **Sceleritas Fel** appears and gives the mantle.
- **WARNING:** costs Alfira (and Withers' Bard tutor scenes); if a companion or someone else kills her, Sceleritas does **not** give the cloak — you must deliver the kill.
- **Do:** Only for an evil/Bhaal-leaning Dark Urge run; otherwise resist the urge.

### 10. Hamarhraft (Waukeen's Rest)
- **Hamarhraft** — Uncommon maul: jumping deals 1d4 Thunder in 3 m on landing; Tenacity.
- In a gilded chest on the top floor of the burning Waukeen's Rest inn (X: -85 Y: 631). If you long rest after saving Florrick/Benryn the fire is out; the chest is gone but the maul is still there.
- **Do:** Climb to the top floor during or after the fire rescue and grab it.

### 11. Mind Flayer Parasite Specimens (Act 1)
- Each **Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen** = 1 Illithid Power point.
- Kill-to-loot True Souls: **Dror Ragzlin, Edowin, Flind, Gut, Minthara, Nere** (WARNING: each requires killing that NPC; Minthara/Gut/Dror kills lock out the goblin-side route).
- Loot/quest: Enclave Library, Druid Grove (X: -445 Y: 17); 3× in the Crèche Y'llek infirmary near the Zaith'isk; 1× from "Raid the Emerald Grove" (evil route); 1× from "Free True Soul Nere" (side with Nere).
- **Do:** Collect them for Illithid powers; Lae'zel needs a Persuasion check before she'll eat one.

## ACT 2

### 12. Moonlantern (Shadow-Cursed Lands)
- **Moonlantern** (Story Item; club-type) — bright light + **Moonshield** (protection from the Shadow Curse in a large radius). Contains the pixie **Dolly Dolly Dolly**.
- Sources:
  1. Ruined Battlefield (X: 0 Y: -15): carried by **Kar'niss** — after the Harper ambush or at Moonrise Towers.
  2. Last Light Inn (X: -63 Y: 172): **Isobel** gives it if the Harpers claim it.
  3. Moonrise Towers: Balthazar's chamber.
- Using **Inspect Moonlantern** frees Dolly → party gets **Pixie Blessing** (curse protection), but the lantern becomes the broken one.
- **MISSABLE:** if Kar'niss reaches Moonrise with the lantern intact, Dolly dies inside it.
- **Do:** Ambush Kar'niss on the road before he reaches Moonrise, then free the pixie unless you need the lantern as a light source.

### 13. Dammon's Flawed Helldusk set (Last Light Inn)
- Dammon at Last Light Inn (X: -33/-35 Y: 164/169). Each Infernal Iron you hand over makes the next piece, in this order:
  1. **Flawed Helldusk Armour** — Rare heavy, AC 18; Piercing −1; melee attackers within 2 m: DC 13 DEX or 1d4 Fire.
  2. **Flawed Helldusk Helmet** — Rare medium helm: +1 CON saves, +2 saves vs spells.
  3. **Flawed Helldusk Gloves** — Rare: weapon attacks +1d4 Fire, unarmed +1d4 Necrotic + Bleeding, +1 STR saves.
- Act 2 Infernal Iron: Reithwin Mason's Guild (X: -105 Y: 18); Gauntlet of Shar opulent chest near Balthazar's area (X: -821 Y: -752); carried by **Yurgir** (X: -647 Y: -758).
- **MISSABLE:** bg3.wiki: crafting is only possible **before entering the Shadowfell**; afterwards Dammon only trades. (Some guides suggest Act 3 Forge of the Nine still forges if Dammon was skipped in Act 2 — verify in-game; treat as Act 2-only.)
- **WARNING:** Infernal Iron is shared with Karlach's engine upgrades — plan 2 for Karlach + up to 3 for the set.
- **Do:** Bring all Infernal Iron to Dammon before the Gauntlet of Shar's final Shadowfell descent.

### 14. Gauntlet of Shar (Shadowheart) — trial rewards and Dark Justiciar set
Entry: Grand Mausoleum (Reithwin) — press the fresco buttons in order **Moonrise Towers → Grief → General** ("splendour, tragedy, infamy"), then ride the Traversal Gem lift down. Mausoleum also has **Vivacious Cloak** (Traveller's Chest, DC 14 Sleight of Hand) and **Lightning Jabber** (Cursed Kuo-Toa Chiefs).

Umbral Gems (need 3 for the altar, 4 exist):
- **Soft-Step Trial** (X: -771 Y: -755): stealth maze → Umbral Gem.
- **Self-Same Trial** (X: -767 Y: -727): kill only your *own* shadow copy (others give the curse "Cheater's Folly") → Umbral Gem + **Killer's Sweetheart** (Very rare ring: after a kill, next attack roll is a crit, 1/LR; set the reaction to "Ask") dropped by the player's copy near the brazier (X: -833 Y: -729).
- **Faith-Leap Trial** (south of the others): invisible bridge — follow the floor mosaic or teleport → Umbral Gem.
- **Yurgir** (X: -652 Y: -776): Orthon carries the 4th gem — kill him, trick him into killing his retinue (DC 14 Insight route), or break his contract (Raphael questline).
- Altar: Verge of Shadows (X: -722 Y: -842) — 3 gems open the way to the Shadowfell.

Silent Library (X: around -772 Y: -741): kill the Dark Justiciars / Librarian, take the book **Teachings of Loss: The Nightsinger**, place it on the "Riddle of the Night" pedestal → riddle door opens:
- **Spear of Night** (Rare +1 spear; story weapon, X: -820 Y: -756). **WARNING:** Shadowheart turns hostile if you enter the Shadowfell without it.
- **Dark Justiciar Half-Plate** (Rare version, medium AC 16; X: -818 Y: -755): adv. CON saves, Stealth adv. in shadow, Shar's Aegis 1/LR.
- **Dark Justiciar Helmet** (Very rare, X: -822 Y: -753, gilded chest behind the altar): +1 saves vs spells, crit range −1 while obscured.
- **Dark Justiciar Gauntlets** (Uncommon version) — on boxes near Yurgir (X: -660 Y: -760): +1 STR saves, weapon attacks +1d4 Necrotic.

Nightsong choice (Shadowfell, X: -604 Y: -1431) — "The Chosen of Shar":
- **Shadowheart kills the Nightsong** → **Shar's Spear of Evening** (Legendary +3; obscured bonuses, Shar's Darkness, see in magical darkness), **Dark Justiciar Half-Plate** (Very rare upgrade: AC 17, Shar's Aegis with damage reduction 2 + necrotic reflect), **Dark Justiciar Gauntlets** (Rare upgrade: +Beckoning Darkness), **Dark Justiciar Boots** (Rare: +1 DEX saves, Shadow Teleportation).
  WARNING: kills Aylin (Dame Aylin & Isobel later lost), Shadowheart stays Sharran.
- **Shadowheart spares the Nightsong** → **Moonlight Glaive** (Rare; +1d4 Radiant, light, Moonlight Butterflies) immediately, and after "Infiltrate Moonrise Towers"/Ketheric defeat talk to Aylin in camp → **Selûne's Spear of Night** (Legendary +3; Moonbeam, Darkvision, adv. on WIS saves & Perception).
- **Do:** Decide by build: Sharran dark-stealth build → kill (4 items); otherwise spare for the Legendary Selûne spear + glaive. Either way bring Shadowheart and the Spear of Night.

### 15. Ketheric Thorm gear (Moonrise Towers → Mind Flayer Colony)
- Items (carried by Ketheric; looted from his corpse in the Mind Flayer Colony fight, X: 861 Y: -23, or disarmed/pickpocketed earlier):

### 17. Dark Urge: Slayer Form

## ACT 3

### 18. House of Hope (Raphael) — Helldusk pieces, Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength, Orphic Hammer
Access (Devil's Fee, Lower City, Helsik X: -37 Y: -10):
1. Pay Helsik 20,000 gp, or DC 25 Intimidation for 10,000 gp, or DC 20 Persuasion to take her deal ("Acquire the Gauntlets for Helsik").
2. Upstairs ritual circle: place **Skull, Coin of Mammon, Diamond, Incense** clockwise and **Infernal Marble** in the centre (components from Helsik's room or found yourself).
3. In the House, talk to **Hope** to get **Debtor's Attire** (disguise prevents alarms while roaming).

Loot:
- **Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength** — Very rare gloves: STR becomes 23, +1 STR saves. Archive north pedestal (X: -6549 Y: 2940). Pressure plate: DC 20 Sleight of Hand to disarm, or swap in an item of equal weight. Helsik deal: you owe her the gauntlets — then DC 10 Intimidation (after mentioning a devil kill) to keep them, DC 10 Deception + pay 20,000 gp instead, or hand them over and pickpocket them back. **WARNING:** breaking the deal outright ends trade with Helsik for that character.
- **Orphic Hammer** — Legendary +3 warhammer, Story Item; adv. on saves vs spells, Unshackling Strike. Archive central pedestal (X: -6543 Y: 2921) behind a magic shield; password is in the **Boudoir safe** (under Raphael's portrait, DC 25 Sleight of Hand; also 5 Soul Coins). Taking it raises the alarm. If you side with Orpheus without it, Raphael offers it for a contract (refusing/no Raphael = game over at that point). Also used to free Lae'zel's sword deal (see §21).
- **Amulet of Greater Health** — Archive south pedestal, pressure-plate alarm.
- **Helldusk Helmet** — Very rare: no crits against you, can't be blinded, +2 saves vs spells, Immolating Gaze. Raphael's Vault (X: -6482 Y: 2939): DC 10 Wisdom to activate the Inert Infernal Gem, DC 20 Arcana on the Infernal Control Gem (one try per character). Vault also has 666 gp + **Staff of Spellpower**.
- **Helldusk Gloves** — Very rare: +1 spell attack/DC (bug: +1 all attacks), weapon +1d6 Fire, Rays of Fire, +1 STR saves. Worn by **Haarlep** in the Boudoir (X: -6478 Y: 2993): play their "game" or kill them.
- **Helldusk Armour** — Legendary heavy AC 21, all damage −3, fire resistance, Fly 1/LR, any class can wear it. **WARNING:** Only by **killing Raphael** (he wears it). Freeing Hope or stealing triggers the alarm; Raphael + 6 Vengeful Cambions + Korrilla (+ possibly Yurgir) attack at the exit.
- Other chests: gilded chest (X: -6495 Y: 2952, DC 15) Angelic Slumber potions; rosewood desk (X: -6518 Y: 2913, DC 10 or Theologist's key).
- **Do:** Go in disguised, take helmet/gloves/gauntlets/amulet quietly, then free Hope and kill Raphael for the armour; plan your Helsik payment option first.

### 19. Helldusk Boots (Wyrm's Rock Fortress)
- **Helldusk Boots** — Very rare: ignore difficult terrain, Infernal Evasion (reaction: pass a failed save), can't be moved, Hellcrawler teleport (2d8 Fire).
- Locked gilded chest on the top floor of Wyrm's Rock Fortress (X: -32 Y: 219; the same chest holds a Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen).
- **WARNING:** taking it **after Gortash's coronation** makes Gortash and the Steel Watch permanently hostile (unless you accepted Gortash's deal and Orin is dead). Take it before the coronation or during the Gortash fight.
- Full **Helldusk set** = Armour (Raphael), Helmet (vault), Gloves (Haarlep), Boots (Wyrm's Rock).

### 20. Wyrmway — Balduran's Giantslayer & Helm of Balduran
- **Balduran's Giantslayer** — Legendary +3 greatsword: double STR mod to damage + advantage vs Large+; Giant Form (+27 temp HP, adv. STR); Topple the Big Folk.
- **Helm of Balduran** — Legendary helm: regen 2 HP/turn in combat, no crits, stun immunity, +1 AC and saves.
- Steps:
  1. Wyrm's Rock Prison, western passage: hit both dragon-head torches with **Lightning** damage (Shocking Grasp, Witch Bolt, Lightning Arrow) — illusion wall drops. (Alt: mousehole on the western cliffs.)
  2. Antechamber Balduran statue: answer that you are worthy → door opens.
  3. Four trials (each lights a statue; failing spawns undead but you can continue):
     - **Justice**: pick the painting "The Cell".
     - **Insight**: catch the flying books (DC 15 Sleight of Hand / spells), read them; the harmful advisor is **Cristina Suelto** — eliminate her.
     - **Strategy**: lanceboard mate-in-two (3 random variants; 3 tries; Gale gives hints) — or destroy the black king with Lightning.
     - **Courage**: one character holds the blue torch and must survive 4 turns vs elementals/myrmidons (Sanctuary/resist potions trivialise it).
  4. Dragon's Sanctum: Ansur's remains → Emperor/Balduran reveal → fight **Ansur** (+2 Water Myrmidons).
  5. Loot: **Balduran's Giantslayer** on Ansur's corpse (X: 633 Y: -989); **Helm of Balduran** on the altar nearby (X: 636 Y: -964).
- **Do:** Bring a Lightning caster; Giantslayer is the best STR greatsword in the game.

### 21. Silver Sword of the Astral Plane (Lae'zel / Kith'rak Voss)
- **Silver Sword of the Astral Plane** — Legendary +3 greatsword; Soulbreaker; githyanki wielders get adv. INT/WIS/CHA saves, psychic resist, charm immunity, +1d6 Psychic.
- Main route: Lower City Sewers (X: -70 Y: 828) — show **Kith'rak Voss** the **Orphic Hammer** (or kill him) → he hands over the sword.
- Alt: DC 30 Deception with Voss during "Deal with the Devil" if you decline Raphael; or (cheese) disarm Voss in Act 1 at the Mountain Pass approach (Command: Drop/Heat Metal/fear; many reloads).
- **WARNING:** killing Voss angers the githyanki/Lae'zel's path.
- **Do:** Get the Orphic Hammer from the House of Hope first, then meet Voss — best on Lae'zel.

### 22. Crown of Karsus & the Netherstones
- **Ketheric's Netherstone** — Act 2/3 Mind Flayer Colony, Ketheric's corpse (X: 861 Y: -23).
- **Gortash's Netherstone** — set in the **Netherstone-Studded Gauntlet** (splits into the gauntlet + stone). Kill Gortash at Wyrm's Rock Fortress, or accept his alliance deal (he dies at the Morphic Pool and the stone transfers).
  - Gortash's other drops: Cloth of Authority, Fabricated Arbalest, Reflectoguard, Tyrannical Jackboots, Phase Optimizer, Gortash's Key/Hand, Note from Helsik.
- **Orin's Netherstone** — in the pommel of **Netherstone-Pommelled Bloodthirst** (splits into **Bloodthirst** dagger + stone). Kill **Orin** at the Temple of Bhaal altar (Undercity Ruins). Access needs the **Amulet of Bhaal** (become an Unholy Assassin at the Murder Tribunal or kill Sarevok). Dark Urge: one-on-one duel. Orin also drops **Crimson Mischief**, **Ring of Murderous Opportunity**, Studded Leather Armour +2, Altar Key.
- **Triune Netherstone** — auto-created from all three at "Confront the Elder Brain"; grants **Karsus' Compulsion**.
- **Crown of Karsus** — not equippable; environmental object on the Netherbrain. Target it with Karsus' Compulsion in the finale (Honour Mode adds Karsite Grip).
- **Do:** Collect all three stones before the finale; no player gear involved beyond the boss drops.

### 23. Gontr Mael (Steel Watch Foundry) — note: *not* a Lae'zel item
- **Gontr Mael** — Legendary +3 longbow: hits apply Guiding Bolt (advantage), Celestial Haste 1/LR, Bolt of Celestial Light.
- Carried by the **Steel Watcher Titan** in the Steel Watch Foundry Control Centre (X: -1952 Y: 206).
- **WARNING:** does **not** drop if the Titan dies from the **Atrophied** condition — kill it normally.
- **Do:** Defeat the Titan during the Foundry sabotage; best bow for any archer.

### 24. Nyrulna (Circus of the Last Days → Chult jungle)
- **Nyrulna** — Legendary +3 trident: returns when thrown, 3d4 Thunder burst (6 m) on throw, +3 m movement & jump, falling-damage immunity.
- Steps:
  1. Circus of the Last Days, Rivington — **Akabi**'s wheel (X: -68 Y: -75).
  2. Win the jackpot by: pickpocketing his **Djinni Ring** (removes his cheat), DC 15 Performance distraction (joke/fart), or DC 20 Perception to catch him.
  3. Akabi teleports the winner **alone** to a jungle in Chult.
  4. Near the exit portal: locked painted chest (X: -1511 Y: -1522), **DC 20 Sleight of Hand** (lockpick) → **Nyrulna** + ring **Band of the Mystic Scoundrel**.
  5. Leave via the portal. Akabi's game closes permanently.
- **WARNING:** the teleported character is alone — send someone who can survive/lockpick (a rogue with Summon-type help).
- Note: Nyrulna is **not** in the Act 1 Zhentarim Hideout (that vault holds gold, potions and Infernal Iron).
- **Do:** Steal Akabi's ring, spin with your best lockpicker, open the chest beside the portal.

- **Hellfire Greataxe** (Very rare +2; +1d6 Fire, Heat, Hellflame Cleave): go through the **Silverhand** door, then *destroy* the door marked **Illusion**; gilded chest (X: 366 Y: 941).
- **Foebreaker** (Very rare +2 maul; ignores Bludgeoning resistance, Tenacity): doors **Silverhand → Abjuration → Silver**, pull the lever for the Karsus vault, return via the Demon door; **invisible** chest (See Invisibility / Faerie Fire) at X: 326 Y: 998.
- **Pyroquickness Hat**: invisible chest in the Elminster vault.
- Legendary books: *The Red Knight's Final Strategem* (X: 368 Y: 1056, DC 20), *The Tharchiate Codex* (X: 416 Y: 998, DC 20; WARNING: curse "Tharchiate Withering" until Remove Curse, then a buff), *The Annals of Karsus* (X: 318 Y: 997).

- No guards or theft penalties in the vault.

- Notable: **Nymph Cloak** (Vault 1), **The Long Arm of the Gur** (Vault 3), **Trident of the Waves** (Vault 4); plus a Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen (X: -713 Y: 881). Vault keys are scattered on NPCs around the city; lockpicking vaults triggers no response.

- **Do:** For necromancer wizards, kill him (after destroying the jars) for the staff.

### 28. Viconia's Walking Fortress (House of Grief)

## ELIXIRS & ALCHEMY (build-defining)

How alchemy works: three identical ingredients → one extract (**Salts, Essences, Ashes, Sublimates, Suspensions, Vitriols**) stored in the Alchemy Pouch ("Extract All Ingredients"). Each elixir = one *specific* extract + any extract of a given type. Drinking an elixir replaces any other active elixir; effects last until long rest.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Elixir of Hill Giant Strength** | Uncommon | STR becomes 21 (never lowers) | Salts of Hill Giant Fingernails (from **Hill Giant Finger**) + any Suspension | Finger: Derryth Bonecloak & Blurg (Myconid Colony). Elixir sold by Auntie Ethel (3/rest, Riverside Teahouse), Derryth, Esther (Rosymorn trail), Araj Oblodra (Moonrise), Mattis & Quartermaster Talli (Last Light), Entharl Danthelon & Oliver Tefoco (Act 3). Found: Ethel's shop display; South Span Checkpoint, Wyrm's Crossing (X: -13 Y: 55) |

### Coatings (brief)
Same two-extract pattern (one specific + one generic): e.g. **Arsonist's Oil**, **Oil of Accuracy**, **Wyvern Toxin**. Applied to weapons for a few turns/until rest; not build-defining.

### Dyes (brief)
Combine a dye with an armour/clothing item to recolour it (cosmetic only; Dye Remover strips it; dyes don't stack). Tiers: Uncommon single colours, Rare two-tone, Very rare premium (e.g. Boreal Blue, Cobalt, Drake General, Sinful Red on Bone White). Sold by general traders and Helsik.

## Items from the request that do NOT exist as multi-step items (to avoid false matches)
- "Diamond-steel" / "Hellsteel" weapons — no such items found on bg3.wiki or guides. Closest: the Adamantine forge items (§1) and Infernal weapons (Hellbeard Halberd, Infernal Spear, Infernal Warhammer — plain loot/Helsik's Curated Selection, no crafting).
- "Duergar mithral" — the only mithral in the game is the 2× **Mithral Ore** for the Adamantine Forge (§1).
- "Gontr Mael (Lae'zel)" — Gontr Mael is the Steel Watcher Titan's longbow (§23); Lae'zel's sword is the Silver Sword of the Astral Plane (§21).
- "Nyrulna (Zhentarim hideout)" — Nyrulna is Act 3 via Akabi (§24); the Zhentarim vault gives Infernal Iron.
- Infernal Armoury — just a lore book on a shelf in Helsik's room (X: -40 Y: 9).
- Helsik's Curated Selection (DC 5 Perception ×n + DC 15 Arcana to unlock): Caitiff Staff, Cloak of the Weave, Gemini Gloves, Hellbeard Halberd, Shapeshifter Hat, Spellseeking Gloves.
