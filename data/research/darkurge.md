# The Dark Urge - LootAdvisor research (Patch 8 + hotfixes)

Researched 2026-10-08. The main source for item data, locations and coordinates is bg3.wiki (X,Y values are bg3.wiki
map coordinates). Build and consensus notes come from Gamestegy, AlcastHQ, GameRant, GameRevolution, dotesports,
Prima, Steam discussions and Game8 (sources are listed at the end).
Reddit could not be fetched directly, so reddit opinion appears only second-hand through Steam threads and guides.
All text is paraphrased.

Conventions:
- `WARNING:` marks theft, killing a neutral NPC or a story choice, and says what it costs.
- `MISSABLE:` marks an item that can be lost for good.
- "LLI" means Last Light Inn (Act 2).
- Item names are the exact in-game English names, as written on bg3.wiki.
- The Durge's race and class are free, like a custom Tav. Every build below works on the Durge.
- Durge-specific twists:
  - The Deathstalker Mantle is the Act 1 cloak for every build.
  - Killing Isobel destroys LLI and its traders.
  - Act 3 Bhaal content (Murder Tribunal, Orin, Sarevok) gives extra Bhaal gear.

## 1. Builds covered

These are ordered by how much the Durge community plays them and how strongly guides recommend them.

1. **Oathbreaker Paladin 12** (`oathbreaker`). The thematic favourite: the oath mirrors the fall-or-redeem story ("Oathbreaker. Period." on Steam). It is a two-handed STR smiter with Aura of Hate. Killing Valeria at the Murder Tribunal costs it nothing, because no oath is left to break.
2. **Sorcadin, Vengeance Paladin 6 / Shadow Sorcerer 6** (`sorcadin`). Rated S+ in the Gamestegy tier list. Quickened Hold Person turns hits into crit smites. Shadow Magic suits the Durge, and Darkness pairs with the Dark Justiciar / obscured-crit gear.
3. **Gloom Stalker 5 / Assassin 4 / Battle Master 3** (`gloomassassin`). The Durge's power pick: the Deathstalker Mantle makes you invisible after a kill, which gives the next shot Advantage and Sneak Attack. In Act 3 Bhaalist Armour makes nearby targets take double piercing damage from arrows.
4. **Throwzerker, Berserker 10 / Fighter 2** (`throwzerker`), with Path of Giants (`giants`) sharing almost the same gear. Popular "embrace the Urge" melee pick for low-WIS builds. It throws piercing weapons (Returning Pike, Nyrulna), which suits Bhaalist Armour, and the Mantle procs on its many kills.
5. **Lockadin, Vengeance Paladin 5 / Hexblade 7** (`lockadin`). Rated S+ in the Gamestegy tier list. CHA-only melee with short-rest slots. Often played as Oathbreaker on the Durge.
6. **Bardadin, Paladin 2 / College of Swords Bard 10** (`bardadin`). Rated S+ in the Gamestegy tier list. Gets the CHA dialogue on the Durge. Duellist's Prerogative + Band of the Mystic Scoundrel + Hold Person lets you smite held targets for automatic crits.
7. **Storm Sorcerer 10 / Tempest Cleric 2** (`stormsorc`). The Durge's default class is Sorcerer; one Steam poster called Storm the "default" Durge. It uses lightning-charge gear in Act 1 and the Reverberation-and-Daze item chain.
8. **Tavern Brawler Open Hand Monk 12** (`tbmonk`). Rated S in the Gamestegy tier list. A low-WIS striker that procs the Mantle on its many kills.

Not covered in depth: the other mod builds (Battle Master Fighter, Light Cleric, Sorlock, Evocation Wizard, Moon Druid, Stars Druid). These are rarely recommended specifically for the Durge. Their gear matches the generic lists for other origin characters, plus the Deathstalker Mantle in Act 1 for any of them.
- Battle Master Fighter: use the Oathbreaker list without the CHA items.
- Sorlock: use the Storm Sorc list without the lightning-charge items, plus Potent Robe.
- Moon Druid: almost no gear matters while in Wild Shape, so Slayer form competes with it.

### 2.2 Bhaal-related gear (not Durge-exclusive, but the Durge story leads there)

The Durge reaches the Bhaal Temple without the Amulet of Bhaal (Sceleritas opens the door). The Murder Tribunal is still where the best Bhaal gear is.
- If the Durge already has Slayer form, Sarevok skips the murder test.
- Without Slayer, the Durge passes the spectre test with DC 10 Deception or DC 15 Intimidation.
- **Sarevok turns hostile if the Durge enters the Tribunal after the duel with Orin.** Do the Tribunal first if you want to trade with the Echo of Abazigal.

Durge Act 3 Orin/Bhaal choice, summed up:
- **Accept Bhaal:**
  - Gain Slayer (if missing), Power Word Kill, and the endgame A Most Bloody Inheritance.
  - Lose Jaheira and Minsc.
  - Get the evil ending, with a forced confrontation with your closest companion or romance in the epilogue.
- **Refuse Bhaal:**
  - The Durge dies and Withers revives you, free of the Urge.
  - Lose Slayer.
  - Gain only the Gibus loot from Sceleritas.
  - Keep every companion and ally.
- **Disobey after accepting:** Bhaal shreds your mind after the final battle (bad epilogue). This has no gear impact.

## 4. Synergy sets (full loadouts per act)

Slot order: Main / Off-hand / Ranged | Helmet | Cloak | Armour | Gloves | Boots | Amulet | Ring 1 | Ring 2.

### Act 1

**A1-1 "First Blood of the Mantle"** (Gloom Stalker / Assassin)
- Loadout: Titanstring Bow (or Longbow +1) + Knife of the Undermountain King | Shadow of Menzoberranzan | The Deathstalker Mantle | Spidersilk Armour | Gloves of Archery | Disintegrating Night Walkers | Amulet of Misty Step | Caustic Band | Strange Conduit Ring.
- Why: open combat invisible with the hood, and Assassinate plus Dread Ambusher crits the opener. Each kill re-cloaks you through the Mantle, so every shot keeps Advantage and Sneak Attack. Every arrow adds +2 (Archery gloves), +2 acid (Caustic Band) and +1d4 psychic (Conduit ring with Hunter's Mark).
- WARNING: Spidersilk comes from Minthara. Swap in Adamantine Scale Mail if you spare her.

**A1-2 "Adamantine Smiter"** (Oathbreaker / Sorcadin / Lockadin)
- Loadout: Unseen Menace (Sorrow before the Crèche) + Bow of Awareness | Diadem of Arcane Synergy | The Deathstalker Mantle | Adamantine Splint Armour | Gloves of the Growling Underdog | Disintegrating Night Walkers | Broodmother's Revenge | Ring of Protection | Caustic Band (Sorcadin/Lockadin: Ring of Arcane Synergy).
- Why: Unseen Menace gives Advantage and crits on 19-20, so smites crit often. Command or Hold Person conditions trigger Diadem damage. The Splint makes you crit-immune and takes 2 off every hit. The Mantle chains invisibility after kills.
- WARNING: the Diadem, Ring of Arcane Synergy and Ring of Protection each come with a fight or theft (see 3A.1).

**A1-3 "Sparkstorm"** (Storm Sorcerer / Tempest)
- Loadout: The Spellsparkler + The Real Sparky Sparkswall | The Lifebringer | The Deathstalker Mantle | The Protecty Sparkswall | Gloves of Belligerent Skies | Boots of Stormy Clamour | The Blast Pendant | Ring of Absolute Force | Ring of Protection.
- Why: every damaging spell builds Lightning Charges (Spellsparkler). The charges power the Sparkswall armour's AC, DC and saves, the Lifebringer's temp HP and the shield's Lightning Aura. Lightning and thunder damage set up Reverberation for the Act 2 Daze ring.
- MISSABLE: the Spellsparkler is 1 of Florrick's 3 rewards.

**A1-4 "Pike Reaper"** (Throwzerker / Giants)
- Loadout: Returning Pike + Bow of Awareness | Haste Helm | The Deathstalker Mantle | Adamantine Scale Mail | Gloves of Uninhibited Kushigo | Disintegrating Night Walkers | Amulet of Misty Step | Ring of Flinging | Caustic Band.
- Why: every throw stacks +1d4 (gloves), +1d4 (ring) and +2 acid on top of Tavern Brawler's doubled STR. Frenzy kills re-cloak you, giving Advantage without Reckless Attack's drawback.
- Giants variant: The Sparkle Hands instead of the Kushigo gloves.

**A1-5 "Spark Fist"** (Tavern Brawler Open Hand Monk)
- Loadout: Unarmed (Club of Hill Giant Strength trick) | Haste Helm | The Deathstalker Mantle | The Graceful Cloth | The Sparkle Hands | Boots of Speed | Sentient Amulet (Rare) | Crusher's Ring | Ring of Protection.
- Why: 4-5 unarmed hits a turn build charges, with Advantage against armoured foes. Speed from the helm, Crusher's Ring and Boots of Speed makes the monk reach anything. Flurry kills trigger invisibility.

### Act 2

Rule for this act: **buy every LLI item before deciding on Isobel.**

**A2-1 "Shadow Hunter"** (Gloom Stalker / Assassin)
- Loadout: Darkfire Shortbow (pre-cast Haste) or Least Expected | Covert Cowl | The Deathstalker Mantle | Yuan-Ti Scale Mail | Gloves of Archery | Disintegrating Night Walkers | Surgeon's Subjugation Amulet | Killer's Sweetheart | Shadow-Cloaked Ring.
- Why: Act 2 is dark everywhere, so you and your targets are obscured. The Cowl crits on 19-20, Least Expected adds +1d4 and the Shadow-Cloaked Ring adds +1d4 against obscured foes. An Assassin crit on a humanoid Paralyses it, so the following shots auto-crit. Killer's Sweetheart guarantees the crit after a kill.

**A2-2 "Justiciar's Darkness"** (Sorcadin; also Oathbreaker)
- Loadout: Halberd of Vigilance | Dark Justiciar Helmet | Cloak of Protection | Reaper's Embrace (or Adamantine Splint Armour before the Colony) | Flawed Helldusk Gloves | Evasive Shoes | Spellcrux Amulet | Killer's Sweetheart | Shadow-Cloaked Ring.
- Why: Shadow Magic's Darkness and the Act 2 gloom keep you obscured, so you crit on 19-20 with doubled smite dice. Flawed Helldusk adds fire to every swing. Spellcrux refunds a top-level slot for one more smite or Haste.

**A2-3 "Thunder Daze Chain"** (Storm Sorcerer / Tempest)
- Loadout: The Spellsparkler + Ketheric's Shield | Fistbreaker Helm | Thunderskin Cloak | Potent Robe | Gloves of Belligerent Skies | Boots of Stormy Clamour | Spellcrux Amulet | Ring of Spiteful Thunder | Ring of Mental Inhibition.
- Why: lightning and thunder hits apply Reverberation (gloves and boots). The next damage forces a CON save or the target is Dazed (Spiteful Thunder), and failed saves add Mental Fatigue. With +2 DC from the helm and shield, Potent Robe's CHA-boosted cantrips and temp HP, it is a control blaster.
- WARNING: Potent Robe needs Alfira saved and LLI standing.

**A2-4 "Oath of Ruin"** (Oathbreaker Paladin)
- Loadout: Harmonium Halberd | Helmet of Arcane Acuity | Cloak of Protection | Armour of Devotion | Flawed Helldusk Gloves | Evasive Shoes | Spellcrux Amulet | Killer's Sweetheart | Ring of Protection.
- Why: +2 STR from the halberd frees ASIs for CHA. Armour of Devotion gives back a Channel Oath, so you get two Dreadful Aspects or Spiteful Sufferings. Acuity lifts Command and Hold Person DCs. Killer's Sweetheart and Spellcrux each give one extra crit-smite.
- WARNING: four of these items are at LLI.

**A2-5 "Duellist's Flourish"** (Bardadin)
- Loadout: Phalar Aluve (empty off-hand) | Helmet of Arcane Acuity | Cloak of Protection | Yuan-Ti Scale Mail | Gloves of The Duellist | Boots of Brilliance | Spellcrux Amulet | Killer's Sweetheart | Risky Ring.
- Why: Duelling style plus the Duellist gloves (+2 attack) and Risky Ring (Advantage) make flourishes land. Boots of Brilliance refund a Bardic Inspiration. Every hit stacks Acuity for Hold Person, then you smite the held target.

### Act 3

**A3-1 "Bhaal's Chosen Smite"** (Oathbreaker / Sorcadin)
- Loadout: Balduran's Giantslayer | Sarevok's Horned Helmet | Cloak of Displacement | Helldusk Armour | Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength | Helldusk Boots | Amulet of Greater Health (Oathbreaker: Amulet of the Devout) | Killer's Sweetheart | Band of the Mystic Scoundrel.
- Why: double STR damage plus crits on 19-20 (Sarevok's helm). If the Durge accepted Bhaal, A Most Bloody Inheritance makes it 17-20 in the finale, and every crit doubles the smite dice. Band of the Mystic Scoundrel turns Hold Person into a bonus action after a hit, so the next swings auto-crit. Helldusk Armour and the Gauntlets make you tanky with no STR investment.
- Lockadin variant: Sword of Chaos, Birthright, Helldusk Gloves.
- WARNING: Raphael fight, Archive theft, Sarevok fight, and Bhaal acceptance for the finale bonus.

**A3-2 "Unholy Assassin"** (Gloom Stalker / Assassin)

- Why: hidden opener crit range 18-20 (Dead Shot +1, Shade-Slayer +1), and Assassin crits are automatic on surprised foes anyway. Paralysis follows on humanoids. Bhaalist Armour doubles piercing damage for enemies within 3 m. +2 attack (Mask) and +2 attack and damage (Legacy) offset Sharpshooter.
- WARNING: Valeria kill (Bhaalist), theft (Mask), Dammon alive (Legacy).

**A3-3 "Murder Throw"** (Throwzerker / Giants)

- Why: Nyrulna's piercing damage is doubled by the Bhaalist aura, and every throw also triggers a thunder blast. Helmet of Grit gives an extra bonus action (an extra Frenzy throw) below half HP. Flat bonuses stack on each of 3-4 throws a turn.
- Giants variant: Bonespike Garb (temp HP when raging) instead of Bhaalist Armour.
- WARNING: Valeria, Viconia.

**A3-4 "Scoundrel's Prerogative"** (Bardadin)
- Loadout: Duellist's Prerogative (empty off-hand) | Helmet of Arcane Acuity | Cloak of Displacement | Bhaalist Armour | Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength | Helldusk Boots | Amulet of Greater Health | Band of the Mystic Scoundrel | Killer's Sweetheart.
- Why: the rapier crits on 19-20 and gets a bonus-action attack. Its piercing damage is doubled by the Bhaalist aura. After a hit, Band makes Hold Person or Hypnotic Pattern a bonus action, at a DC already raised by Acuity, then you smite the held target for automatic crits.
- WARNING: Valeria, Archive theft. MISSABLE: Lora.

**A3-5 "Weave Tempest"** (Storm Sorcerer / Tempest)
- Loadout: Markoheshkir (lightning) + Ketheric's Shield | Hood of the Weave | Cloak of the Weave | Potent Robe | Gloves of Belligerent Skies | Boots of Stormy Clamour | Amulet of the Devout | Ring of Spiteful Thunder | Ring of Free Action.
- Why: +5 spell DC from items for Chain Lightning and Lightning Bolt, a free spell from Arcane Battery, and Reverberation into Daze from the same lightning hits. Amulet of the Devout gives a second Destructive Wrath, which maximises lightning and thunder damage.
- WARNING: Tabernacle curse; Potent Robe depends on Alfira.

## 5. Community consensus notes

### Which builds people play on the Durge
- The **thematic favourite** is **Oathbreaker Paladin**, alone or as the paladin half of a Lockadin or Sorcadin. GameRant calls Paladin the most fitting class for the redeem-or-fall story. A Steam thread asking for the best Durge class gets "Oathbreaker. Period." among its top answers, along with Berserker Barbarian (unique lines), CHA classes (Sorcerer, Bard) and Storm Sorcerer. The Durge's default class is Sorcerer.
- The **power favourites** are stealth-crit builds that abuse **The Deathstalker Mantle**: Assassin Rogue, Gloom Stalker/Assassin, Swashbuckler and throwers. dotesports ranks Rogue first precisely because of the Mantle (invisible = Advantage = Sneak Attack every turn). AlcastHQ's "Best Dark Urge Build" (Patch 8) is a Swashbuckler with Bhaalist gear, Crimson Mischief and Bloodthirst.
- GameRevolution's advice:
  - Embracing the Urge suits low-WIS martial classes; the Slayer form and Mantle reward them.
  - Resisting means many WIS saves, so pick WIS-save classes (Paladin, Warlock, Druid, Wizard) or Resilient/Lucky.
- Gamestegy lists The Deathstalker Mantle as the Act 1 cloak, marked "Dark Urge only", on its Oathbreaker, Sorcadin, Lockadin, Bardadin, Throwzerker, Giant Barbarian and Open Hand Monk pages. On the Gloom Stalker/Assassin page it keeps the Mantle as best in slot through Act 3.
- One Gamestegy page describes the Mantle as a crit bonus. That is wrong; it gives invisibility on a kill.

### Items
- **Bhaalist Armour** is the most common Act 3 chest piece for piercing builds (archers, rapiers, Nyrulna and Pike throwers, Shar's Spear). AlcastHQ's paladin builds keep Adamantine Splint Armour instead, and most others pick Helldusk Armour for heavy-armour paladins. It costs Valeria's life (or a risky pickpocket).
- **Slayer form:** players find it fun but weaker than a finished build late in the game; Prima says it beats the Owlbear but loses to a Moon Druid's Panther. It is once per long rest. Its usual price, the Isobel kill and Last Light falling, is considered steep: you lose the LLI traders, Dammon's Act 3 forge (Armour of Persistence, Legacy of the Masters, Boots of Persistence) and the Potent Robe.
- **Power Word Kill** (one use, 100 HP cap) is considered weak. **A Most Bloody Inheritance** (party-wide crit -2 plus Stunning Gaze in the finale) is the real combat reward for accepting Bhaal.
- **Gibus of the Worshipful Servant** is free for the Durge but appears in no build guide.
- "Gauntlets of Frost Giant Strength", used by Gamestegy, is not a real item name. The in-game item is **Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength** (STR 23).
- "Cloak of Billowing", used by Gamestegy, does not exist on bg3.wiki. Use Cloak of Displacement.
- Several guides pair **Broodmother's Revenge** with **Ring of Regeneration**: the ring heals every turn, so the +1d6 poison stays on permanently.

- Gamestegy (Jul 2026, Honour Mode basis):

  - B: Giant Barbarian

### Where guides disagree
- Class splits:
  - Throwzerker is 10/2 here; Gamestegy uses 5/3/3 and Alcast 5/4/3.
  - Gloom Stalker's fourth class is BM 3 here; others use Fighter 2 / War Cleric 1.
  - Sorcadin's sorcerer subclass varies between Shadow and Draconic.
- Act 3 chest: Bhaalist Armour, Helldusk Armour or Adamantine Splint Armour.
- Strength source: Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength or keeping Legacy of the Masters.
- Act 1 cloak: Gamestegy always uses the Mantle for the Durge; Alcast never mentions it.
