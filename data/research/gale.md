# LootAdvisor research — GALE (Human, Wizard by default) — BG3 Patch 8 + hotfixes

Written in our own words from bg3.wiki (primary source for names, effects and coordinates),
cross-checked against build guides (Gamestegy, eip.gg, Arzyel, exputer, Game8/Fextralife
snippets). Reddit itself could not be fetched (crawler blocked); community consensus is
taken from guide sites that summarise it. Source URLs at the end.

Conventions (same as `crafted.md`)
- **Item names** are written exactly as bg3.wiki spells them (intended to match game data).
- Coordinates are bg3.wiki "X: Y:" map coordinates (House of Hope / Ramazith's Tower use
  their own coordinate frame).
- **WARNING:** = theft, killing a neutral NPC, hostility, or a story choice with a cost.
- **MISSABLE:** = timing window / mutually exclusive reward.
- Builds tags: **EVO** = Evocation Wizard 12 (main), **STORM** = Storm Sorcerer 10 /
  Tempest Cleric 2, **FIRE** = fire-focused wizard (Arcane Acuity), **COLD** = cold/control,
  **BLADE** = Bladesinging Wizard (Patch 8 subclass), **ABJ** = Abjuration wizard,
  **CTRL** = Divination/Enchantment/Illusion controller wizard, **ALL** = any Gale caster.

### Gale-specific rules the advisor must know
1. **Human = Light Armour + Shields proficiency** (Civil Militia; also spears, pikes,
   halberds, glaives). So even pure-Wizard Gale can use **Ketheric's Shield**, any
   shield, and light armour such as **Armour of Landfall**. No medium/heavy without a dip.
2. **STORM** (Sorcerer start + 2 Cleric): cleric multiclass adds medium armour + shields,
   Tempest Domain adds heavy armour + martial weapons. Main stat is **Charisma**
   (Cleric levels only for Destructive Wrath + armour; Wisdom items are wasted).
3. **Bladesong** is impeded by medium/heavy armour **and shields**, and needs a
   dagger / longsword / rapier / scimitar / shortsword / sickle in main hand — so BLADE
   loadouts never use Ketheric's Shield or staves.
4. Gale can **consume** many magic items (his Act 1–2 orb hunger). Wiki flags these as
   "consumable by Gale": Circlet of Blasting, Psychic Spark, The Protecty Sparkswall,
   The Spellsparkler, The Sparkswall, The Blast Pendant, The Real Sparky Sparkswall,
   Disintegrating Night Walkers, Bracers of Defence, Haste Helm, Amulet of Misty Step,
   Robe of Summer, Hood of the Weave. Advisor should warn: **never feed a BiS item**.
5. Flat spell-DC bonuses stack with each other and with **Arcane Acuity** (+1 per
   remaining turn, max 10, loses 2 turns every time the wearer takes damage).
   Patch 8 changed **Gloves of Battlemage's Power**: they now only trigger on
   weapon-delivered spells (Booming Blade, smites, Shadow Blade, Flame Blade…), so they
   are a BLADE item, no longer an EVO item.

## 1. Builds covered

- **EVO — Evocation Wizard 12** (mod main): INT, Sculpt Spells AoE (Fireball, Ice Storm,
  Chain Lightning via staff), Empowered Evocation at 10 → wants flat DC/attack, spell-slot
  restoration, concentration protection.
- **STORM — Storm Sorcerer 10 / Tempest Cleric 2** (mod alt): CHA, Destructive Wrath
  maximises lightning/thunder, Wet + Lightning; wants lightning/thunder riders, CHA items,
  extra Channel Divinity, shield + medium/heavy armour.
- **FIRE — Fire Evoker variant**: Scorching Ray / Fireball spam with Hat of Fire Acuity
  Arcane Acuity stacking (community-favourite Gale build).
- **COLD — Cold control / Abjuration variant** (Gamestegy "Abjuration Wizard 10 /
  Tempest 1 / Draconic 1"): Ray of Frost, Ice, Encrusted with Frost, Armour of Agathys.
- **BLADE — Bladesinging Wizard** (new in Patch 8): Booming Blade + Gloves of
  Battlemage's Power Arcane Acuity, light armour, no shield.
- **CTRL — Divination/Enchantment controller**: Hold/Confusion/Hypnotic; wants pure
  DC, Mental Fatigue/Daze on failed saves.
- **Wizard dips** seen in guides: Wizard 10 + Cleric 1–2 (armour/Shield, Tempest), Wizard +
  Sorcerer 1–2 (CON save proficiency, Shield) — these use the EVO/STORM lists below.

## 4. Synergy sets (loadouts)

### Act 1
1. **"Sparkstruck Missiles"** — STORM / EVO. Main The Spellsparkler; Off The Real Sparky
   Sparkswall; Head The Lifebringer; Chest The Protecty Sparkswall; Amulet Psychic Spark;
   Rings The Sparkswall + Ring of Protection; Boots Disintegrating Night Walkers.
   *Why:* every Magic Missile dart (4+ with Psychic Spark) gives 2 Lightning Charges →
   Sparkswall robe +1 AC/saves, Lifebringer temp HP, shield aura dump; Sparkswall ring
   stops self-electrocution.
2. **"Reverberation Lockdown"** — STORM. Gloves of Belligerent Skies + Boots of Stormy
   Clamour + Ring of Absolute Force (Thunderwave) + The Spellsparkler + The Protecty
   Sparkswall + The Shadespell Circlet. *Why:* lightning/thunder hits and every
   condition stack Reverberation → −CON saves, Prone at 5 stacks; sets up Act 2
   Ring of Spiteful Thunder.
3. **"Evoker DC"** — EVO / CTRL. Melf's First Staff + Adamantine Shield + The Shadespell
   Circlet + The Protecty Sparkswall + Daredevil Gloves + Disintegrating Night Walkers +
   Pearl of Power Amulet + Ring of Protection + Ring of Absolute Force.
   *Why:* +2 DC (+3 in shadow), spare slot restore, escape button, crit-proof shield.
4. **"Frostbite Control"** — COLD / ABJ. Mourning Frost + Winter's Clutches + Disintegrating
   Night Walkers (no slipping on own ice) + Necklace of Elemental Augmentation (Ray of
   Frost + INT) + Haste Helm + Infernal Robe only if Karlach path (else Protecty
   Sparkswall). *Why:* cold spells → Chilled + Encrusted with Frost; ice surfaces safe for you.
5. **"Cantrip Blaster"** — EVO / FIRE. Necklace of Elemental Augmentation + Daredevil
   Gloves + Circlet of Blasting + Melf's First Staff + Bracers of Defence (if no shield).
   *Why:* Fire Bolt gets +INT; Daredevil removes melee-range disadvantage.

### Act 2
1. **"Fire Acuity Evoker"** — FIRE / EVO. Incandescent Staff (or Melf's First Staff) +
   Ketheric's Shield + Hat of Fire Acuity + The Protecty Sparkswall + Cloak of Protection
   + Daredevil Gloves + Disintegrating Night Walkers + Spellcrux Amulet + Ring of Free
   Action + Ring of Mental Inhibition. *Why:* upcast Scorching Ray → each fire beam adds
   Acuity turns → next Fireball at +5–10 DC; Spellcrux refunds the big slot.
   (WARNING: Hat requires killing the Strange Ox.)
2. **"Radiant Orb Storm"** — STORM. Luminous Armour + Coruscation Ring + Callous Glow Ring
   + Gloves of Belligerent Skies + Boots of Stormy Clamour + Spineshudder Amulet +
   Thunderskin Cloak + Ketheric's Shield + The Spellsparkler. *Why:* spells apply
   Radiating Orb → targets illuminated → Callous Glow adds Radiant per hit → Luminous
   Armour shockwave + Belligerent Skies Reverberation; Thunderskin dazes attackers.
3. **"Charisma Storm"** — STORM. The Spellsparkler + Ketheric's Shield + Fistbreaker Helm
   + Potent Robe + Cloak of Protection + Gloves of Belligerent Skies + Boots of Stormy
   Clamour + Spellcrux Amulet + Ring of Spiteful Thunder + Callous Glow Ring + Ne'er
   Misser. *Why:* Gamestegy Storm Sorcerer Act 2 core (+2 flat DC, CHA cantrip damage,
   free 3rd-level Magic Missile each short rest).
4. **"Glacier"** — COLD. Mourning Frost + Coldbrim Hat + Winter's Clutches + Snowburst
   Ring + Disintegrating Night Walkers + Necklace of Elemental Augmentation. *Why:* every
   cold hit drops ice and Encrusted with Frost; any condition (Coldbrim) adds more frost.
5. **"Bladesong Acuity"** — BLADE. Gloves of Battlemage's Power + Hat of Storm Scion's
   Power + Ring of Elemental Infusion + Strange Conduit Ring + The Protecty Sparkswall
   (or light armour) + Disintegrating Night Walkers; **no shield**. *Why:* Booming Blade
   is weapon-delivered (Battlemage) and Thunder (Storm Scion) → two Acuity sources.

### Act 3
1. **"Weave Archmage" (max flat DC)** — EVO / CTRL. Markoheshkir + Ketheric's Shield +
   Hood of the Weave + Robe of the Weave + Cloak of the Weave + Helldusk Gloves +
   Helldusk Boots + Amulet of Greater Health + Ring of Feywild Sparks + Ring of Free
   Action + Elixir of Battlemage's Power. *Why:* +7 flat DC/+6 attack (staff 1, shield 1,
   hood 2, robe 1, cloak 1, gloves 1, ring 1 DC) + elixir +3 → INT-20 Gale ≈ DC 27;

2. **"Devout Landfall"** — EVO / CTRL. As set 1 but Armour of Landfall (CON-save
   advantage) instead of Robe of the Weave, and Amulet of the Devout (+2 DC) instead of
   Amulet of Greater Health. *Why:* same concentration protection, +2 DC more. WARNING:
   Devout needs the Tabernacle workaround.
3. **"Inferno Acuity"** — FIRE. Markoheshkir (Flame of Wrath) + Ketheric's Shield + Hat of
   Fire Acuity + Robe of the Weave + Cloak of the Weave + Spellmight Gloves + Helldusk
   Boots + Spellcrux Amulet + Ring of Feywild Sparks + Ring of Free Action + Elixir of
   Battlemage's Power. *Why:* Scorching Ray beams build Acuity and Spellmight adds 1d8 per
   beam (the −5 is offset by Acuity); Flame of Wrath adds +prof fire damage and Heat.
4. **"Chain Lightning Storm"** — STORM. Markoheshkir (Bolts of Doom) + Ketheric's Shield
   + Hood of the Weave (or Birthright) + Potent Robe + Cloak of the Weave + Gloves of

   Ring of Spiteful Thunder. *Why:* Devout = second Destructive Wrath (maximised Chain
   Lightning on Wet targets); Reverberation → Daze; +5 flat DC.
5. **"Spellblade"** — BLADE. Rhapsody + Gloves of Battlemage's Power (or Helldusk Gloves)
   + Hat of Storm Scion's Power + Band of the Mystic Scoundrel + Ring of Elemental
   Infusion + Armour of Landfall (light) + Cloak of Displacement + Helldusk Boots +
   Amulet of Greater Health; no shield. *Why:* weapon hits feed Acuity/Rhapsody DC, bonus-
   action enchantment/illusion after a hit, CON advantage for concentration.

## 5. Community consensus notes

- **Undisputed BiS (Act 3):** Markoheshkir (weapon), Hood of the Weave, Robe of the Weave,
  Cloak of the Weave, Helldusk Boots, Ketheric's Shield for any shield-capable caster —
  Human Gale qualifies. Staff of Spellpower is the standard "second caster" staff.
- **Amulet debate:** Amulet of Greater Health (concentration/HP, safest for Wizard, no
  CON-save proficiency) vs Amulet of the Devout (+2 DC; must-have for Tempest/Channel
  Divinity builds) vs Spellcrux Amulet (Gamestegy Storm and Arzyel pick). Armour of
  Landfall lets you take Devout without losing CON advantage.
- **Hat of Fire Acuity vs Hood of the Weave:** eip.gg treats Hood as the Act 3
  replacement; Game8-style fire guides keep the Hat with Markoheshkir for fire spam
  (Acuity can exceed +2 but drops 2 per hit taken). Advisor: Hood default, Hat for FIRE.
- **Act 1 weapon:** Gamestegy (Storm, Abjuration) picks The Spellsparkler; eip.gg picks
  Melf's First Staff. Both valid — Spellsparkler for damage, Melf's for save DC.
  Gamestegy even runs Melf's First Staff in the off hand with Spellsparkler main hand.
- **Act 1 robe:** The Protecty Sparkswall is the near-universal pick (+1 DC + lightning
  AC). Infernal Robe appears in older guides but costs Karlach — advisor should not
  recommend it unless that path happened.
- **Reverberation/Radiating Orb sets** (Belligerent Skies, Stormy Clamour, Callous Glow,
  Coruscation, Spiteful Thunder, Luminous Armour) are widely used for Storm/Tempest and
  thunder casters; they are niche for a pure fire Evoker.
- **Patch 8:** Gloves of Battlemage's Power reworked (weapon-delivered spells only) —
  older guides that recommend them for Evocation Gale are outdated. Bladesinging is the
  new popular Gale alternative; Bladesong forbids shields and medium/heavy armour.
- **Disagreements / errors found in sources:**
  - Several low-quality sites (exputer, AI-generated aggregator pages) list items such as
    "Staff of Crones", "Ring of Fire", "Circlet of Fire", "Poisoner's Robe" that were
    **not verified** on bg3.wiki in this pass — do not match them without checking.
  - Arzyel lists Hat of Fire Acuity under Act 1 — it is only obtainable from Act 2.
  - eip.gg recommends Mage's Friend (+1 Arcana/Religion) and Sunwalker's Gift (darkvision)
    rings — minor utility, not combat BiS.
  - bg3.wiki item summaries sometimes tag Moonrise / Last Light / Crèche items as "Act 3"
    in their act field; actual acts: Crèche Y'llek, Lady Esther, Grymforge, Underdark =
    Act 1; Moonrise Towers, Last Light Inn, Reithwin, Gauntlet of Shar = Act 2.
  - Cloak of Protection: rarity shows "Uncommon" on its item page but "Rare" in the cloak
    list — use game data.
