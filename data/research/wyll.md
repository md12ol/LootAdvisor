# LootAdvisor research — WYLL (Human, locked) — BG3 Patch 8

Scope: best items per act and slot for Wyll's builds. Written in our own words from bg3.wiki
(primary source for item data and locations), cross-checked against build guides and community
threads (URLs at the end). Researched 2026-10.

Conventions (same as `crafted.md`)
- **Item names** are written exactly as bg3.wiki spells them (intended to match game data).
- Coordinates are bg3.wiki "X: Y:" map coordinates; sub-levels (Crèche, House of Hope, Gauntlet of
  Shar, Jungle, Ramazith's Tower) use their own coordinate frame.
- **WARNING:** = theft, killing a neutral NPC/creature, broken oath, lost companion or other story cost.
- **MISSABLE:** = timing window or one-time chance.
- Build tags: **SL** = Sorlock (main), **LP** = Lockadin, **EB** = pure Warlock Eldritch Blast,
  **HB** = Hexblade melee, **BL** = Bladelock / Booming-Blade gish.

Wyll-specific proficiency facts that change what he can use
- Human "Civil Militia": light armour, **shields**, spears, pikes, **halberds**, glaives — so even the
  Sorlock can carry a shield (Ketheric's Shield / Adamantine Shield) with no feat.
- Wyll's passive "The Blade of Frontiers" keeps **rapier** proficiency through any respec → Infernal
  Rapier / Duellist's Prerogative are always usable.
- Shortbows are simple weapons → every Warlock build is proficient (Bow of Awareness, Darkfire Shortbow).
  Longbows are martial (HB/LP proficient; SL/EB still get passive effects from the ranged slot).

## 1. Builds covered

1. **Sorlock (Fiend Warlock 2 / Draconic Sorcerer 10)** — main BuildAdvisor build. Red Draconic
   (fire). Agonising + Repelling Blast from the dip, Quickened Eldritch Blast = 2 casts per turn;
   Hex for extra damage; CHA is the only key stat. Gear priorities: CHA, damage per beam, spell attack.
2. **Lockadin (Vengeance Paladin 5 / Hexblade Warlock 7)** — CHA melee: Hex Warrior makes weapon
   attacks use CHA, Pact of the Blade binds a two-hander, short-rest Warlock slots fuel Divine Smite.
   Gear priorities: heavy armour, crit/advantage, extra weapon damage riders, CHA.
3. **Pure Warlock Eldritch Blast (Fiend or Hexblade Warlock 12)** — same item core as Sorlock
   (Potent Robe etc.) but no Quickened; Quickspell Gloves / Gemini Gloves matter more.
4. **Hexblade melee (Hexblade 5+ with Pact of the Blade, shield or two-hander)** — overlaps Lockadin
   items without the smite focus.
5. **Bladelock / gish (Pact of the Blade + Booming Blade, Infernal Rapier)** — Arcane Synergy and
   Arcane Acuity items (cantrip → weapon damage loops).
   (Variant seen in guides: crit-fishing EB with Warlock 2 / Sorcerer 6 / Fighter 4 — noted in §5.)

## 2. Best items per act and slot

### ACT 1

#### Main hand
- **The Spellsparkler** — Rare quarterstaff (versatile). Every time you deal damage with a spell or
  cantrip you gain 2 Lightning Charges (each EB beam counts separately). Charges add +1 lightning
  damage (and, per bg3.wiki, +1 to attack rolls) each, and at 5 charges they detonate for an extra
  1d8 lightning. Why: turns every beam into a lightning proc, which also triggers Gloves of
  Belligerent Skies (Reverberation) and powers The Protecty Sparkswall. Builds: **SL, EB**.
  Get: Waukeen's Rest (X: -63, Y: 600) — rescue Counsellor Florrick from the burning inn
  ("Rescue the Grand Duke"); she immediately offers one of three rewards: pick The Spellsparkler
  (others: The Joltshooter, The Sparky Points). **MISSABLE:** timed fire; only one of the three.
- **Melf's First Staff** — Uncommon +1 quarterstaff; +1 spell save DC and spell attack rolls;
  Melf's Acid Arrow once per long rest. Why: plain +1 to hit on every beam. Builds: **SL, EB**
  (runner-up). Get: sold by Blurg, Ebonlake Grotto (Myconid Colony, Underdark) X: 111, Y: -89, ~100 gp.
- **The Blood of Lathander** — Legendary +3 mace (simple weapon, one-handed). Light that blinds
  fiends/undead, once-per-long-rest self-revive with ally heal, Sunbeam (lvl 6) once per long rest.
  Why: +3 one-hander that pairs with a shield; Hex Warrior uses CHA with it. Builds: **LP, HB**
  (also a fine SL melee fallback). Get: Crèche Y'llek secret chamber, locked altar (X: 1068,
  Y: -779) — quest "Find the Blood of Lathander": rotate the Lathander statues in the left corridor of
  the Inquisitor's Chamber to open the passage.
- **Phalar Aluve** — +1 longsword, finesse/versatile (bg3.wiki item page says Rare, list page says
  Very rare). Melody: Sing (allies +1d4 attacks) or Shriek (enemies −1d4). Builds: **HB, LP, BL**.
  Get: Underdark X: 116, Y: -192, stuck in a rock — DC 15 Strength or DC 15 Religion (Paladins DC 10
  with advantage), or spill blood on the stone.
- **The Baneful** — Rare +1 shortsword; only when it is your Pact weapon / Hexblade-bound does it
  work (+1 more to hit/damage, Bane on hit). Builds: **BL, HB**. Get: Blurg, Ebonlake Grotto
  X: 111, Y: -89.
- **Everburn Blade** — greatsword 2d6 + 1d4 fire, earliest magic two-hander. Builds: **LP** (pact-bind).
  Get: Commander Zhalk on the Nautiloid (X: -53, Y: -391). **MISSABLE:** prologue only.

## 4. Synergy sets

### Act 1
1. **Spark Battery** (SL, EB) — Main: The Spellsparkler · Off: Adamantine Shield · Ranged: Bow of
   Awareness · Head: Haste Helm · Chest: The Protecty Sparkswall · Gloves: Gloves of Belligerent Skies ·
   Boots: Boots of Stormy Clamour · Amulet: Amulet of Misty Step · Rings: Ring of Protection, Crusher's
   Ring. Why: every beam adds Lightning Charges → constant +AC/saves from the Sparkswall and lightning
   procs; lightning damage (gloves) and casting Hex (boots) both apply Reverberation, which already
   knocks enemies prone at high stacks and sets up the Act 2 daze rings.
2. **Steady Aim** (EB, SL) — Main: Melf's First Staff · Gloves: Daredevil Gloves · Head: The Shadespell
   Circlet · Amulet: Pearl of Power Amulet · rest as set 1. Why: +2 to EB to-hit early, Daredevil removes
   the point-blank disadvantage; Devil's Sight + Darkness keeps the Circlet's DC bonus active.
3. **Adamantine Smiter** (LP) — Main: The Blood of Lathander · Off: Adamantine Shield · Chest: Adamantine
   Splint Armour · Head: Helmet of Smiting · Gloves: Gloves of the Growling Underdog · Boots: Boots of
   Speed · Amulet: Amulet of Misty Step · Rings: Strange Conduit Ring, Caustic Band. Why: uses both Mithral
   Ore for an uncrittable tank; concentration on Hex/Bless powers the Conduit ring; each of the 1–2 attacks
   carries +1d4 psychic and +2 acid; smite conditions refill temp HP.
4. **Arcane Synergy Gish** (BL, HB) — Main: Phalar Aluve or The Baneful (pact-bound) · Chest: Adamantine
   Scale Mail · Head: Diadem of Arcane Synergy · Rings: Ring of Arcane Synergy, Strange Conduit Ring.
   Why: Booming Blade / Hex / Hexblade's Curse trigger Arcane Synergy, adding CHA to weapon damage a
   second time on top of Hex Warrior.

### Act 2
1. **Daze Lock** (SL, EB) — Main: The Spellsparkler · Off: Ketheric's Shield · Chest: Potent Robe · Cloak:
   Thunderskin Cloak · Gloves: Gloves of Belligerent Skies · Boots: Boots of Stormy Clamour · Amulet:
   Spineshudder Amulet · Rings: Ring of Spiteful Thunder, Risky Ring (or Callous Glow Ring). Why: the
   first beam applies Reverberation (amulet), every later beam forces a DC 13 CON save or Dazed (ring);
   enemies that then hit you roll again via the cloak. Dazed = no reactions, no DEX to AC. Weak spot:
   the DC is fixed at 13, so it falls off against high-CON late bosses.
2. **Radiant Orb** (EB, SL) — Chest: Potent Robe · Gloves: Luminous Gloves · Rings: Callous Glow Ring,
   Coruscation Ring · Amulet: Spineshudder Amulet. Why: Coruscation lights the target with Radiating Orb,
   Callous Glow then adds +2 radiant to every beam, and that radiant damage re-applies Radiating Orb
   (gloves) — or Reverberation if you wear Belligerent Skies instead.
3. **Fire Acuity Sorlock** (SL, Red Draconic) — Main: Incandescent Staff · Off: Ketheric's Shield · Head:
   Hat of Fire Acuity · Chest: Potent Robe · Amulet: Necklace of Elemental Augmentation (or Spellcrux
   Amulet) · Rings: Risky Ring, Ring of Mental Inhibition. Why: open with Fire Bolt (CHA added by the
   necklace and by Elemental Affinity) to stack Arcane Acuity, then Quickened EB / Hold Person benefit
   from the stacked +hit/+DC.
4. **Crit Smiter** (LP) — Main: Halberd of Vigilance (2H) or Infernal Rapier + Shield of Devotion · Chest:
   Adamantine Splint Armour · Cloak: Cloak of Protection · Gloves: Flawed Helldusk Gloves · Boots: Evasive
   Shoes · Amulet: Surgeon's Subjugation Amulet · Rings: Risky Ring, Killer's Sweetheart. Why: advantage
   from Risky Ring roughly doubles crit chance, Killer's Sweetheart guarantees one, a crit on a humanoid
   paralyses it (amulet) so following melee hits auto-crit — all doubling smite dice.

### Act 3
1. **Max-CHA Blaster** (SL, EB — consensus BiS) — Main: Markoheshkir · Off: Ketheric's Shield · Ranged:
   Hellrider Longbow · Head: Birthright · Chest: Potent Robe · Cloak: Cloak of the Weave · Gloves:
   Spellmight Gloves · Boots: Helldusk Boots · Amulet: Amulet of Greater Health · Rings: Risky Ring,
   Callous Glow Ring. Why: CHA 22 (24 with Mirror of Loss) is added twice per beam (Agonising + Potent
   Robe); Risky Ring's advantage offsets Spellmight's −5 for +1d8 per beam; +1/+1 from staff, shield and
   cloak keep hit chance high.
2. **Endgame Daze Lock** (SL, EB) — Set 1 but swap Gloves → Gloves of Belligerent Skies, Boots → Boots of
   Stormy Clamour, Amulet → Spineshudder Amulet, a ring → Ring of Spiteful Thunder, Cloak → Thunderskin
   Cloak. Why: crowd control instead of raw damage; good on trash packs, weaker vs bosses (DC 13).
3. **Fire Sorlock Caster** (SL, Red) — Main: Markoheshkir · Head: Hat of Fire Acuity · Chest: Robe of the
   Weave (or Potent Robe) · Gloves: Helldusk Gloves · Cloak: Cloak of the Weave · Amulet: Spellcrux Amulet
   · Rings: Ring of Mental Inhibition, Risky Ring. Why: stack Arcane Acuity with fire, then land Hold
   Monster/Fireball; Mental Inhibition chains failed saves.
4. **Infernal Lockadin** (LP) — Main: Balduran's Giantslayer (pact-bound) or Blackguard's Sword +
   Viconia's Walking Fortress · Ranged: Hellrider Longbow · Head: Birthright (or Helm of Balduran) · Chest:
   Helldusk Armour · Cloak: Cloak of Displacement · Gloves: Helldusk Gloves · Boots: Helldusk Boots ·
   Amulet: Amulet of Greater Health · Rings: Band of the Mystic Scoundrel, Killer's Sweetheart. Why: hit →
   bonus-action Hold Person/Monster (ring) → paralysed target → every hit crits with smites; Helldusk
   pieces add flat fire per hit and huge damage reduction.
5. **Hexblade Duellist** (HB, BL) — Main: Duellist's Prerogative (empty off-hand) or Infernal Rapier ·
   Head: Mask of Soul Perception (theft) or Helmet of Arcane Acuity · Chest: Armour of Agility · Gloves:
   Legacy of the Masters · Boots: Helldusk Boots · Amulet: Amulet of Greater Health · Rings: Ring of Arcane
   Synergy, Band of the Mystic Scoundrel. Why: Booming Blade → Arcane Synergy (+CHA to weapon damage again);
   crit on 19 plus Hexblade's Curse (crit on 19 → 18 with the Rapier) maximises crits.

## 5. Community consensus notes

- **Potent Robe** is near-universally called BiS for any Eldritch Blast Wyll, from Act 2 to the end;
  most guides keep it over Robe of the Weave for EB because the CHA rider hits every beam.
- **Birthright** is the consensus head for all CHA builds (Warlock, Sorlock, Lockadin). Hood of the Weave is
  a niche alternative for pure save/attack casters.
- **Markoheshkir** is the consensus best caster weapon; Staff of Spellpower / Caitiff Staff are runners-up.
- **Helldusk set (Armour, Gloves, Boots)** is the standard Lockadin/heavy-armour endgame; one guide
  (hacktheminotaur) instead lists Armour of Agility for Lockadin — that only beats heavy armour with high
  DEX, which our Lockadin (DEX 14) lacks.
- **Daze lock (Spineshudder Amulet + Ring of Spiteful Thunder + Boots of Stormy Clamour + Thunderskin
  Cloak + Gloves of Belligerent Skies)** is a popular, cheap Act 2 combo (Gamestegy rates Spineshudder BiS);
  others call it trash-pack control that fades vs high-CON bosses because the DC is fixed at 13.
- **Risky Ring + Spellmight Gloves** is a widely cited EB damage pair; Callous Glow Ring is the common second
  ring. Risky Ring's save disadvantage is the known downside (pair with Amulet of Greater Health/Cloak of
  Protection).
- **Balduran's Giantslayer for Lockadin** — sources disagree whether the "double Strength" applies to CHA
  when pact-bound; bg3.wiki only mentions Strength.
- **Craterflesh Gloves** (Gamestegy: bug fires an extra EB on crits) — unverified, and buying them needs the
  Unholy Assassin route (Dark Urge), so effectively unavailable to Wyll; excluded.
- **Level split**: our data uses Paladin 5 / Hexblade 7; hacktheminotaur recommends Paladin 7 / Warlock 5
  (Aura of Protection). Gamestegy's EB build is Warlock 2 / Sorcerer 6 / Fighter 4 (crit-fishing); our
  Sorlock is Warlock 2 / Sorcerer 10.
- **Our BuildAdvisor gear lines**: Sorlock "Hat of Fire Acuity, Markoheshkir, Ring of Mental Inhibition,
  Callous Glow Ring" matches research (but Potent Robe and Birthright are missing and are the two most
  important items). Lockadin "Helldusk Armour, Sword of Justice, Birthright, Band of the Mystic Scoundrel" —
  **Sword of Justice is only an Uncommon +1 greatsword** (Act 1, Anders / killing Karlach) and is
  outclassed; suggest Balduran's Giantslayer or Blackguard's Sword instead.
- **Where sources disagree on data**: rarity of Phalar Aluve, Cloak of Protection, Ketheric's Shield and
  Adamantine Shield differs between bg3.wiki list pages and item pages; Gamestegy puts Melf's First Staff in
  Act 2 although its vendor (Blurg) is in the Act 1 Underdark; some guides call the Potent Robe an Act 1 item
  (it is the Act 2 Alfira reward).
- **Wyll-specific**: Infernal Rapier (free Mizora, DC 14 Persuasion) is the only Wyll-exclusive reward worth
  chasing; Infernal Robe needs Karlach killed; Duke Ravengard's Longsword (+2 CHA) can only be taken from
  his father (theft, Wyll may turn hostile).
